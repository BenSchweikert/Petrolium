Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to me: ben@beert.de. It helps to mention your device, the iOS version and – for bugs – what you did just before.

Frequently asked questions

Is my game saved?

Yes, automatically after every turn. Choose “Continue” on the title screen to resume.

A minigame is too hard for me.

In the settings you can switch on “More time (+50%)” or “Resolve automatically” for each minigame. During a minigame, the “Resolve automatically” button does the same.

How do I see the secretary's tips again?

Settings → “Show tutorials again”. The tips appear again in the next new game.

Can several people play?

Yes, up to four people on one device, taking turns (hotseat). Seats can also be filled with computer opponents.

Accessibility

Petrolium supports VoiceOver, large text and Reduce Motion. With VoiceOver, every minigame asks before it starts whether you want to play it or have it resolved automatically.
