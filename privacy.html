2. The Petrolium app

Petrolium does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The app contains no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no sign-in. Saved games and settings are stored only locally on your device and never leave it. Player names you enter also stay on the device.

Deleting the app deletes all saved games and settings. Backups of your device (e.g. iCloud Backup) follow your settings with Apple.

Statistics and crash reports from Apple: If you agreed in the iOS settings under “Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements” to share with app developers, Apple provides me with aggregated usage figures (e.g. number of installations) and crash reports. Apple collects this data under its own responsibility; I only see it aggregated or without reference to you and use it to fix bugs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can stop sharing at any time in the iOS settings.

Beta version (TestFlight): If you test Petrolium before release via TestFlight, Apple passes on to me the name and email address you were invited with, as well as crash reports and feedback you send through TestFlight. I use them only for the test (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them when the test has ended.

3. App Store

Apple handles the purchase and download of the app through the App Store and processes data under its own responsibility; see Apple's Privacy Policy. I do not receive any personal data about you in the process.

4. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time and the page requested, in order to deliver the site. According to GitHub, visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and available website). GitHub decides how long it keeps this data; I have no access to it.

Data is transferred to the USA. GitHub takes part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Art. 45 GDPR) and additionally uses the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Details: GitHub's Privacy Statement.

The website sets no cookies, loads no external fonts or scripts and uses no analytics.
