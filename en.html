Petrolium

A turn-based oil business game for iPhone and iPad, for 1 to 4 players on one device or against the computer.

It is 1950 and you found a small oil company. You buy drilling rights in fictional regions, order surveys, drill, build tanks and pipelines, and sell your oil through supply contracts or on the spot market – while surviving fires, crises and your rivals.

Features

  • One month is one turn: few decisions, each of them weighty.
  • Five fictional regions from the wheat plains to the storm shelf, plus the deep sea from 1970.
  • Short skill minigames: drilling, pipe network, firefighting and night shift. Each can also be resolved automatically.
  • Computer opponents with their own personalities.
  • Optional extensions: research, reputation and environment, auctions, insurance, achievements and chronicle.
  • Pixel art and original music, with modern controls: system font, VoiceOver and large text.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Languages: German and English.
